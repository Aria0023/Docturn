# Staff accounts

A staff account is for nurses, technologists, pharmacists and other
non-physician staff. A director adds one from Directory → People → Add person →
Nurse / staff, with a job title ("Charge nurse, ED"). An ER director can add
and manage staff accounts too. SSO group mapping and SCIM accept the role.

## What staff can do

- Message anyone, including urgent and STAT messages, and patient threads.
- See the directory and who is on call.
- Follow admissions: their home screen lists every patient not yet accepted,
  who was asked, how long ago and when the request lapses, and the accepted
  patients with their hospitalist. It updates live.
- Run the built-in reports and build private ones.
- Be put on a code team, be alerted, acknowledge, mark arrival, tick steps,
  close a code, and call a code.
- Receive broadcasts, including ones addressed to "Nurses and staff".

## What staff cannot do

Add or edit patients, send, accept, decline or reassign an admission, request
or answer a consult, send a broadcast, manage people, protocols, the rotation
or any setting, see director analytics, or share a report with the
organization. The server refuses each of these; the menu does not offer them.

## Billing

A staff account counts as one seat, the same as any other account. If nurses
should be priced differently from physicians, that is a pricing decision to
make before a hospital adds many of them.

## Demo

The demo has a charge nurse: sign in as `nurse`, or choose "Charge nurse
(staff)" in the Demo as switcher.
