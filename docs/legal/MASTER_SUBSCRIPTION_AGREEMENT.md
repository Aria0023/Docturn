This Master Subscription Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of [DATE]
(the "Effective Date") between **[DOCTURN LEGAL ENTITY NAME]**, a [STATE]

## 1. Definitions

**"Service"** means DocTurn's hosted clinical communication and
patient-assignment software, its web and mobile applications, and its
documentation. **"Order"** means an order form signed by both parties that
references this Agreement and states the subscription term, number of users
("Seats"), fees and modules. **"Users"** means Customer's employees,
contractors and medical staff whom Customer authorises to use the Service.
**"Customer Data"** means all information Customer and its Users submit to the
Service, including PHI. **"BAA"** means the Business Associate Agreement
between the parties, incorporated into this Agreement.

## 2. The Service

(a) **Access.** Subject to this Agreement and payment of fees, DocTurn grants
Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right during the subscription term
to permit its Users to use the Service for Customer's internal clinical and
operational purposes.

(b) **Seats.** Each active User account is one Seat. Seats are counted daily as
set out in the Order. Use above the committed Seats is invoiced as overage; it
is never used to restrict clinical messaging, alerts, admission routing or
sign-in.

(c) **Support and availability.** DocTurn provides support and availability as
described in the Service Level Agreement (Exhibit B).

(d) **Changes.** DocTurn may improve or modify the Service but will not
materially reduce its core functionality during a paid subscription term.

## 3. Customer responsibilities

Customer is responsible for: (a) its Users' compliance with the DocTurn Terms
of Use, Acceptable Use Policy and Clinical Safety Notice; (b) granting,
reviewing and removing User access, roles and on-call schedules; (c) the
accuracy of information it and its systems provide; (d) all clinical
decisions, which remain the responsibility of its licensed professionals;
(e) **maintaining independent backup means of communication for urgent and
emergency situations**; (f) obtaining any consents and providing any notices
required for its use of the Service; and (g) the configuration choices it
makes, including message retention periods and integrations it enables.

## 4. Fees and payment

(a) Customer will pay the fees in each Order. Invoices are due **[net thirty
(30)] days** from the invoice date.

(b) Fees exclude taxes; Customer pays applicable sales and similar taxes other
than taxes on DocTurn's income.

(c) Overdue amounts may bear interest at the lesser of **[1.0]%** per month or
the maximum lawful rate. If an undisputed amount remains unpaid **[thirty (30)]
days** after notice, DocTurn may decline to add new Seats until it is paid.
**DocTurn will not suspend clinical messaging, alerts, admission routing or
sign-in for non-payment** without first giving Customer **[sixty (60)] days'**
written notice and an opportunity to arrange an orderly transition.

## 5. Customer Data and privacy

(a) **Ownership.** As between the parties, Customer owns Customer Data.

(b) **Use.** DocTurn will use Customer Data only to provide, secure and
support the Service, as the BAA permits, and as required by law. DocTurn will
not sell Customer Data, use it for advertising, or use PHI to train
artificial-intelligence models.

(c) **De-identified and usage data.** DocTurn may collect and use aggregated or
de-identified data about the operation of the Service, which does not identify
Customer, any User or any Individual, to operate and improve the Service
[NEGOTIABLE].

(d) **BAA.** The BAA governs PHI. If this Agreement conflicts with the BAA
regarding PHI, the BAA controls.

(e) **Subprocessors.** DocTurn's current subprocessors are listed at
`/subprocessors`. DocTurn will give at least **[thirty (30)] days'** notice of
a new subprocessor that may access PHI; Customer may object on reasonable
data-protection grounds, and if the parties cannot resolve the objection,
Customer may terminate the affected Service and receive a pro-rata refund of
prepaid fees.

## 6. Security

DocTurn will maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards
designed to protect Customer Data consistent with the HIPAA Security Rule, as
summarised in Exhibit A (Security Overview), including encryption in transit,
access controls, audit logging, and the ability to require multi-factor
authentication. DocTurn will maintain a written security risk analysis and
incident response plan, and will notify Customer of incidents as the BAA
requires.

## 7. Confidentiality

Each party will protect the other's Confidential Information (non-public
information marked or reasonably understood as confidential, including the
terms of this Agreement and DocTurn's non-public product and security
information) with at least reasonable care, use it only to perform this
Agreement, and disclose it only to personnel and advisers who need to know and
are bound by similar obligations. Standard exclusions apply (public,
previously known, independently developed, rightfully received). Disclosure
required by law is permitted with prompt notice where lawful. PHI is governed
by the BAA.

## 8. Intellectual property

DocTurn owns the Service and all improvements to it. Customer will not
reverse engineer, copy, resell or create derivative works of the Service.
DocTurn may use feedback without restriction. No rights are granted except as
expressly stated.

## 9. Warranties and disclaimers

(a) **Mutual.** Each party warrants it has authority to enter into this
Agreement.

(b) **DocTurn.** DocTurn warrants that during the subscription term the
Service will perform materially in accordance with its documentation. Customer's
exclusive remedy for breach of this warranty is for DocTurn to use reasonable
efforts to correct the non-conformity and, if it cannot within **[thirty (30)]
days**, for Customer to terminate the affected Service and receive a pro-rata
refund of prepaid fees for the remaining term.

(c) **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED IN THIS AGREEMENT, THE SERVICE IS
PROVIDED "AS IS", AND DOCTURN DISCLAIMS ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR
IMPLIED, INCLUDING MERCHANTABILITY, FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE AND
NON-INFRINGEMENT. DOCTURN DOES NOT WARRANT THAT THE SERVICE WILL BE
UNINTERRUPTED OR ERROR-FREE OR THAT EVERY MESSAGE OR NOTIFICATION WILL BE
DELIVERED. THE SERVICE IS NOT AN EMERGENCY SYSTEM, IS NOT A MEDICAL DEVICE,
AND DOES NOT PROVIDE MEDICAL ADVICE OR MAKE CLINICAL DECISIONS.

## 10. Indemnification

(a) **By DocTurn.** DocTurn will defend Customer against any third-party claim
alleging that the Service, as provided by DocTurn, infringes a United States
patent, copyright or trademark or misappropriates a trade secret, and pay the
resulting damages finally awarded or agreed in settlement. This does not apply
to claims arising from Customer Data, combinations with items DocTurn did not
provide, or modifications DocTurn did not make. If such a claim is made,
DocTurn may obtain the right for Customer to continue, modify the Service to
be non-infringing, or terminate and refund prepaid fees for the remaining
term.

(b) **By Customer.** Customer will defend DocTurn against any third-party claim
arising from (i) Customer Data or Customer's or its Users' use of the Service
in breach of this Agreement or law, or (ii) clinical care provided by
Customer or its Users, and pay the resulting damages finally awarded or agreed
in settlement. [NEGOTIABLE — hospitals resist (ii); it is the single most
important protection for DocTurn, because a missed message is most likely to
surface as a malpractice claim against the clinician and hospital.]

(c) **Procedure.** The indemnified party must give prompt notice, sole control
of the defence and settlement to the indemnifying party (no settlement
admitting fault without consent), and reasonable cooperation.

## 11. Limitation of liability

(a) **Exclusion.** EXCEPT FOR EXCLUDED CLAIMS, NEITHER PARTY WILL BE LIABLE FOR
ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, EXEMPLARY OR PUNITIVE
DAMAGES, OR LOST PROFITS, REVENUE OR GOODWILL, EVEN IF ADVISED OF THEIR
POSSIBILITY.

(b) **General cap.** EXCEPT FOR EXCLUDED CLAIMS AND DATA PROTECTION CLAIMS, EACH
PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT WILL NOT
EXCEED THE FEES PAID AND PAYABLE BY CUSTOMER UNDER THIS AGREEMENT IN THE
**TWELVE (12) MONTHS** BEFORE THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO LIABILITY.

(c) **Data protection cap ("super cap").** DOCTURN'S TOTAL LIABILITY FOR CLAIMS
ARISING FROM A BREACH OF SECTION 6, THE BAA, OR THE CONFIDENTIALITY OF PHI
("DATA PROTECTION CLAIMS") WILL NOT EXCEED THE GREATER OF **[TWO (2)] TIMES**
THE FEES PAID AND PAYABLE IN THE PRECEDING TWELVE (12) MONTHS OR
**[US $____ — set at or below your cyber-insurance limit]**. Within that cap,
DocTurn's liability includes the reasonable, documented costs of notifications
to Individuals and regulators that the law requires as a result of a Breach
caused by DocTurn. [NEGOTIABLE]

(d) **Excluded Claims** — not limited by (a)–(c): a party's gross negligence,
wilful misconduct or fraud; Customer's payment obligations; and each party's
obligations under Section 10 [NEGOTIABLE — many vendors cap indemnities at the
super cap].

(e) The parties agree these limits are an essential basis of the bargain and
reflect the fees charged.

## 12. Term and termination

(a) **Term.** This Agreement continues while any Order is in effect. Each Order
renews for successive **[one-year]** terms unless either party gives notice of
non-renewal at least **[sixty (60)] days** before the end of the term.

(b) **For cause.** Either party may terminate for the other's material breach
not cured within **[thirty (30)] days** after written notice, or if the other
becomes insolvent.

(c) **Transition and data.** For **[thirty (30)] days** after termination,
DocTurn will make Customer Data available for export in a standard format, and
then delete it as the BAA requires, except records DocTurn must keep by law.

(d) **Survival.** Sections 4 (unpaid fees), 5, 7, 8, 9(c), 10, 11, 12(c) and 14
survive.

## Exhibit A — Security Overview

Summarise only what is true on the production deployment at signing. As of
this draft the software provides: TLS encryption in transit; salted scrypt
password hashing; organization-configurable multi-factor authentication
(required by default for privileged roles on real-data deployments); single
sign-on (OIDC) and SCIM provisioning; 15-minute inactivity sign-out and app
lock; per-organization data separation enforced on every query; role-based
access; audit logging of every access to PHI, retained six years;
content-free push and text notifications; configurable message retention; and
a server that refuses to start in real-data mode without its required
safeguards. Encryption at rest is provided by the production database and
storage service. Attach the current risk analysis summary and penetration-test
letter when available. Do not list controls the deployment does not have.
