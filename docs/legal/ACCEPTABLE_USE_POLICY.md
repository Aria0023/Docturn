# DocTurn Acceptable Use Policy

**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]

This policy applies to every user of the DocTurn Service and is part of the
Terms of Use and every Customer Agreement. You may not, and may not help anyone
else to:

1. **Access what you are not allowed to.** Look at patient information you do
   not need for your work, use another person's account, share your account,
   or try to reach data belonging to another organization.
2. **Misuse patient information.** Enter more patient information than is
   necessary; send patient information to anyone not involved in the patient's
   care or operations; copy it out of DocTurn into personal apps, personal
   email, or unapproved storage; or take screenshots of it except as your
   organization permits.
3. **Use DocTurn for emergencies alone.** Rely on DocTurn instead of your
   facility's emergency channels (see the Clinical Safety Notice).
4. **Send harmful or unlawful content.** Harass, threaten or discriminate
   against anyone; send content that is illegal, defamatory or infringes
   someone's rights; or send malware or files designed to harm a device.
5. **Interfere with the Service.** Probe, scan or test it for vulnerabilities
   without DocTurn's written permission; bypass security, rate limits or
   access controls; overload it; or reverse engineer it except where the law
   allows despite this restriction.
6. **Use it outside its purpose.** Use DocTurn for advertising, marketing to
   patients, or any purpose other than your organization's clinical and
   operational communication; resell or sublicense it; or use it to build a
   competing product.
7. **Automate it without permission.** Use bots, scrapers or scripts against
   the Service other than through integrations DocTurn provides and your
   organization has enabled.

**Reporting.** Report suspected misuse, a lost device, or a message sent to the
wrong person to your organization's administrator and to DocTurn at
[SECURITY EMAIL] immediately.

**Enforcement.** DocTurn and your organization may investigate suspected
violations, suspend or end access, and report conduct to law enforcement or a
licensing body where the law requires or permits. A violation involving
patient information may also be a HIPAA breach that your organization must
assess and report.
