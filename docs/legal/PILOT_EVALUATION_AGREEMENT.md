# Pilot and Evaluation Agreement

This Pilot and Evaluation Agreement ("Pilot Agreement") is made as of [DATE]
between **[DOCTURN LEGAL ENTITY NAME]**, a [STATE] [ENTITY TYPE] ("DocTurn"),
and **[PILOT ORGANIZATION LEGAL NAME]** ("Organization").

## 1. The pilot

(a) DocTurn will make its clinical communication and patient-assignment
service (the "Service") available to the users Organization designates (up to
**[NUMBER]** users) for evaluation, from [START DATE] until [END DATE]
(**[NUMBER] days**) unless ended earlier under Section 8 (the "Pilot Term").

(b) The purpose of the pilot is for Organization to evaluate whether the
Service meets its needs. It is not a production deployment.

## 2. No fees; no other remuneration

The Service is provided **free of charge** during the Pilot Term. Neither party
pays the other anything, and nothing of value is exchanged, in connection with
the pilot. No party is obligated to purchase, refer, recommend or arrange for
any item or service. Any later purchase will be under a separate written
agreement at fair market value, negotiated at arm's length. [ATTORNEY: confirm
this language against the Anti-Kickback Statute and Stark Law if DocTurn's
owner is employed by or refers patients to Organization.]

## 3. Data used in the pilot — choose one

[ ] **Option A — Synthetic data only (recommended for a first pilot).**
Organization's users will enter only made-up patient information. **No
Protected Health Information ("PHI") will be entered into the Service.** The
Service displays a "SYNTHETIC DATA — testing only" banner on every screen.
Organization is responsible for instructing its users accordingly. If PHI is
entered in error, Organization will notify DocTurn promptly so it can be
deleted, and the parties will cooperate to assess it under HIPAA.

[ ] **Option B — Real patient information.** Before any PHI is entered, the
parties will sign the attached Business Associate Agreement, and DocTurn will
operate the Service on infrastructure covered by a business associate
agreement with its hosting provider and with the safeguards required for real
patient data enabled.

## 4. Organization's responsibilities

Organization will (a) choose and supervise its users and ensure they accept the
DocTurn Terms of Use, Clinical Safety Notice and Acceptable Use Policy;
(b) **keep its existing paging, on-call and emergency communication processes
in place throughout the pilot** — the Service is used alongside them, not
instead of them; (c) comply with its own policies and applicable law; and
(d) tell DocTurn promptly about any problem that could affect patient safety.

## 5. Feedback

Organization may give DocTurn suggestions, comments or ideas about the Service
("Feedback"). DocTurn may use Feedback for any purpose without restriction or
payment. Feedback does not include Organization's confidential information or
PHI.

## 6. Ownership and confidentiality

(a) DocTurn owns the Service and all intellectual property in it, including
improvements made during or as a result of the pilot. Organization receives no
licence except to use the Service during the Pilot Term for evaluation.

(b) Each party will keep the other's non-public information confidential, use
it only for the pilot, and protect it with at least reasonable care, for
**[two (2)] years** after the pilot ends (and, for trade secrets, for as long
as they remain trade secrets). This does not apply to information that is
public, already known, independently developed or rightfully received from
someone else, or that must be disclosed by law (with prompt notice where
allowed).

## 7. No warranty; limitation of liability

(a) THE SERVICE IS AN EVALUATION SERVICE PROVIDED "AS IS" AND "AS AVAILABLE",
WITHOUT WARRANTY OF ANY KIND, AND MAY CONTAIN ERRORS. DOCTURN DOES NOT WARRANT
THAT IT WILL BE AVAILABLE, UNINTERRUPTED OR ERROR-FREE, OR THAT ANY MESSAGE OR
NOTIFICATION WILL BE DELIVERED. THE SERVICE IS NOT AN EMERGENCY SYSTEM AND DOES
NOT MAKE CLINICAL DECISIONS.

(b) EXCEPT FOR BREACH OF SECTION 6(b), GROSS NEGLIGENCE OR WILFUL MISCONDUCT,
NEITHER PARTY WILL BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL OR
PUNITIVE DAMAGES, AND DOCTURN'S TOTAL LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF THE PILOT WILL
NOT EXCEED **[ONE THOUSAND U.S. DOLLARS (US $1,000)]**. [ATTORNEY: if Option B
is chosen, align with the BAA and consider the insurance-backed amount.]

## 8. Ending the pilot

Either party may end the pilot at any time, for any reason, on written notice
(email is enough). When it ends, Organization's access stops and, within
**[thirty (30)] days**, DocTurn will delete Organization's data from the
Service (or return it first, if Organization asks before the end date and
Option B applies), except records DocTurn must keep by law.
