This Business Associate Agreement ("BAA") is entered into as of [DATE] (the

## 1. Definitions

Capitalised terms used but not defined here have the meanings given in the
HIPAA Rules (45 CFR Parts 160 and 164, as amended by the HITECH Act), including
*Breach, Data Aggregation, Designated Record Set, Disclosure, Health Care
Operations, Individual, Minimum Necessary, Notice of Privacy Practices,
Protected Health Information ("PHI"), Required by Law, Secretary, Security
Incident, Subcontractor, Unsecured Protected Health Information* and *Use*.
"PHI" here means PHI that Business Associate creates, receives, maintains or
transmits on behalf of Covered Entity, including electronic PHI.

## 2. Obligations and activities of Business Associate

Business Associate agrees to:

(a) not Use or Disclose PHI other than as permitted or required by this BAA or
as Required by Law;

(b) use appropriate safeguards, and comply with Subpart C of 45 CFR Part 164
(the Security Rule) with respect to electronic PHI, to prevent Use or
Disclosure of PHI other than as provided for by this BAA;

(c) report to Covered Entity any Use or Disclosure of PHI not provided for by
this BAA of which it becomes aware, including Breaches of Unsecured PHI as
required by 45 CFR §164.410, and any Security Incident of which it becomes
aware, as follows:
  * a Breach of Unsecured PHI: without unreasonable delay and no later than
    **[five (5) business days]** after discovery, and in any case within
    sixty (60) calendar days, with the identification of each affected
    Individual and the other information Covered Entity needs for its
    notifications under 45 CFR §164.404(c), as it becomes available
    [NEGOTIABLE — hospitals commonly ask for 24–72 hours for initial notice];
  * any other non-permitted Use or Disclosure: within **[ten (10) business
    days]** of discovery;
  * Security Incidents: the parties agree that this Section constitutes notice
    of the ongoing existence of unsuccessful Security Incidents (such as pings,
    port scans, failed log-in attempts and denial-of-service attempts that do
    not result in unauthorised access, Use or Disclosure), for which no further
    notice is required;

(d) in accordance with 45 CFR §§164.502(e)(1)(ii) and 164.308(b)(2), ensure
that any Subcontractor that creates, receives, maintains or transmits PHI on
behalf of Business Associate agrees in writing to the same restrictions,
conditions and requirements that apply to Business Associate with respect to
that PHI;

(e) to the extent Business Associate maintains PHI in a Designated Record Set,
make it available to Covered Entity within **[fifteen (15)] days** of a
request, as necessary to satisfy Covered Entity's obligations under
45 CFR §164.524; [Note: DocTurn conversation records are generally not part of
the Designated Record Set; the medical record is the EHR.]

(f) to the extent Business Associate maintains PHI in a Designated Record Set,
make any amendment directed or agreed to by Covered Entity under
45 CFR §164.526, within **[fifteen (15)] days** of a request;

(g) maintain and make available, within **[fifteen (15)] days** of a request,
the information required for Covered Entity to provide an accounting of
disclosures under 45 CFR §164.528;

(h) to the extent Business Associate carries out any of Covered Entity's
obligations under Subpart E of 45 CFR Part 164 (the Privacy Rule), comply with
the requirements of Subpart E that apply to Covered Entity in performing that
obligation;

(i) make its internal practices, books and records relating to PHI available
to the Secretary for purposes of determining compliance with the HIPAA Rules;
and

(j) not receive remuneration in exchange for PHI, and not use PHI for
marketing or for training artificial-intelligence models, except as the HIPAA
Rules expressly permit and Covered Entity authorises in writing.

## 3. Permitted uses and disclosures by Business Associate

(a) Business Associate may Use or Disclose PHI only as necessary to perform the
services set out in the Agreement (the DocTurn clinical communication and
patient-assignment service).

(b) Business Associate may Use or Disclose PHI as Required by Law.

(c) Business Associate agrees to make Uses, Disclosures and requests for PHI
consistent with Covered Entity's Minimum Necessary policies and procedures.

(d) Business Associate may not Use or Disclose PHI in a manner that would
violate Subpart E of 45 CFR Part 164 if done by Covered Entity, except as set
out in (e)–(g).

(e) Business Associate may Use PHI for its proper management and
administration or to carry out its legal responsibilities.

(f) Business Associate may Disclose PHI for its proper management and
administration or to carry out its legal responsibilities, provided the
Disclosure is Required by Law, or Business Associate obtains reasonable
assurances from the recipient that the information will remain confidential
and be Used or further Disclosed only as Required by Law or for the purposes
for which it was disclosed, and that the recipient will notify Business
Associate of any instance in which the confidentiality of the information has
been breached.

(g) Business Associate may provide Data Aggregation services relating to the
Health Care Operations of Covered Entity, and may de-identify PHI in accordance
with 45 CFR §164.514(a)–(c) and Use de-identified information to operate and
improve the service. [NEGOTIABLE — some hospitals strike (g).]

## 4. Obligations of Covered Entity

Covered Entity shall (a) notify Business Associate of any limitation in its
Notice of Privacy Practices, (b) of any change in, or revocation of, an
Individual's permission, and (c) of any restriction on the Use or Disclosure of
PHI it has agreed to under 45 CFR §164.522, in each case to the extent it may
affect Business Associate's Use or Disclosure of PHI; and shall not request
Business Associate to Use or Disclose PHI in any manner that would not be
permissible under Subpart E of 45 CFR Part 164 if done by Covered Entity.
Covered Entity is responsible for configuring the service (users, roles,
retention period, integrations it enables) consistently with its own HIPAA
policies.

## 5. Term and termination

(a) **Term.** This BAA is effective on the Effective Date and remains in effect
until the Agreement ends and all PHI is returned or destroyed under (c).

(b) **Termination for cause.** Covered Entity may terminate this BAA and the
Agreement if Business Associate has violated a material term of this BAA and
has not cured the violation within **[thirty (30)] days** of written notice,
or immediately if cure is not possible.

(c) **Obligations on termination.** On termination, Business Associate shall,
at Covered Entity's election and within **[thirty (30)] days**, return or
destroy all PHI it still maintains in any form (including Subcontractors'
copies), retaining no copies, except that where return or destruction is
infeasible — including records Business Associate must keep under the HIPAA
Rules' documentation-retention requirements, such as audit logs — Business
Associate shall extend the protections of this BAA to that information and
limit further Uses and Disclosures to the purposes that make return or
destruction infeasible, for so long as it is maintained.

(d) **Survival.** Section 5(c) survives termination.

## 6. Miscellaneous

(a) **Regulatory references.** A reference to a section of the HIPAA Rules
means the section as in effect or as amended.

(b) **Amendment.** The parties agree to amend this BAA as necessary for
compliance with changes to the HIPAA Rules.

(c) **Interpretation.** Any ambiguity shall be resolved to permit compliance
with the HIPAA Rules.

(d) **No third-party beneficiaries.** Nothing in this BAA confers rights on
anyone other than the parties.

(e) **Relationship to the Agreement; liability.** If this BAA conflicts with
the Agreement on the protection of PHI, this BAA controls. Each party's
liability under this BAA is subject to the limitation of liability in the
Agreement, including its separate cap for data-protection claims.
[NEGOTIABLE — hospitals often seek uncapped or separately capped liability for
Breach-notification costs; see MSA §11.]

(f) **Offshore access.** Business Associate will not permit PHI to be stored
or accessed outside the United States without Covered Entity's prior written
consent.

(g) **Counterparts; electronic signature.** This BAA may be signed in
counterparts and electronically.
