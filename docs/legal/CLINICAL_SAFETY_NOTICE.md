# DocTurn Clinical Safety Notice

**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]

[DRAFT FOR ATTORNEY REVIEW — NOT IN FORCE]

Please read this before using DocTurn in patient care. It is part of the
DocTurn Terms of Use and of every DocTurn customer agreement.

1. **DocTurn is not an emergency system.** Do not rely on DocTurn alone to
   summon help for a patient in immediate danger. Use your facility's overhead
   paging, rapid-response or code team, or call 911. A STAT message is a
   priority message, not an emergency response.

2. **Delivery is not guaranteed.** Messages, admission requests, consult
   requests and notifications travel over networks, phones and services DocTurn
   does not control. A message can be delayed or not delivered because a phone
   is off, out of signal, in Do Not Disturb or Focus mode, has notifications
   turned off, or because a network or DocTurn itself is unavailable. If you do
   not get a timely answer to anything time-sensitive — including a STAT
   message, an admission request or a consult — **use another channel.**
   Your organization must keep a backup way to reach clinicians.

3. **DocTurn does not make clinical decisions.** DocTurn routes, records and
   displays information. Who should admit a patient, which consultant to call,
   triage levels it suggests from an intake note, and any field it fills in
   automatically are aids for a licensed professional, who remains responsible
   for verifying them and for every clinical decision. DocTurn is not intended
   to diagnose, treat, cure, mitigate or prevent any disease, and it is not a
   medical device.

4. **Accuracy depends on what is entered.** Census figures, on-call schedules,
   shift status and patient details come from your organization, its
   scheduling and health-record systems, and its users. DocTurn shows what it
   was given; check anything that matters against the source.

5. **DocTurn is not the medical record.** Conversations in DocTurn may be
   deleted under your organization's retention settings. Anything that belongs
   in the patient's chart must be documented in the electronic health record.

6. **Keep your device secure.** Use a passcode or biometric lock, keep your
   phone's software up to date, do not share your account, and sign out of
   shared computers. Report a lost device to your administrator at once so
   your sessions can be ended.

7. **Report problems.** If DocTurn behaves in a way that could affect patient
   safety, tell your organization and DocTurn at [SUPPORT EMAIL] immediately.
