This Privacy Policy explains how **[DOCTURN LEGAL ENTITY NAME]** ("DocTurn",
"we", "us") handles information when you visit our website, use the DocTurn web
application, or use the DocTurn mobile apps (together, the "Service").

DocTurn is a secure clinical communication and patient-assignment platform
sold to hospitals and medical groups ("Customers"). Clinicians use it because
their organization has given them an account.

## 1. Two kinds of information, two sets of rules

**Patient information (Protected Health Information, "PHI").** When clinicians
use DocTurn they enter information about patients — for example initials, room
number, reason for admission, triage level, clinical messages and attachments.
DocTurn processes that information **only on behalf of the Customer** that
provides the account, as that Customer's *business associate* under the Health
Insurance Portability and Accountability Act ("HIPAA"). How that information is
used and disclosed is governed by HIPAA and by the Business Associate Agreement
between DocTurn and the Customer — not by this Privacy Policy. Patients with
questions about their information should contact the hospital or medical group
that treated them; that organization's Notice of Privacy Practices applies.

**Information about you as a user.** Everything else in this Policy is about
information about you as a clinician or visitor: your account, your device and
how you use the Service.

## 2. Information we collect about users

| What | Examples | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Account details | Your name, username, organization, role (for example hospitalist or ER physician), professional credential (MD, DO, PA, NP), and optionally a phone number | Your organization's administrator, your organization's identity provider (single sign-on / SCIM), or you when you request an account |
| Sign-in security | Your password (stored only as a salted one-way hash), two-factor authentication settings and backup codes, an optional app-unlock PIN (stored as a hash), session records | You |
| Work activity | Your shift status, the patients routed to or accepted by you, the consults requested of you, your messages and acknowledgements | Your use of the Service |
| Audit records | What you did and which records you opened, with the time, your network (IP) address and your device's browser or app type | Generated automatically, as HIPAA requires |
| Device and notification data | A push-notification token for your device, your notification and sound preferences, your chosen theme | Your device, when you allow notifications |
| Support communications | What you tell us when you contact support | You |

**What we do not collect.** We do not use advertising identifiers, third-party
analytics, tracking pixels or advertising cookies. We do not collect your
location, contacts, photos (other than attachments you choose to send in a
message), microphone (other than voice notes you choose to record) or health
data from your phone's health apps.

## 3. Cookies and similar technology

DocTurn uses one **strictly necessary** cookie to keep you signed in. It
expires after a period of inactivity set by your organization (15 minutes by
default). The web app also stores a few non-identifying preferences in your
browser (for example that you dismissed the "install the app" prompt). No
patient information is stored in your browser's local storage. Because we use
no advertising or analytics cookies, there is nothing to opt out of; blocking
the sign-in cookie prevents you from signing in.

## 4. How we use information about users

* To provide the Service: sign you in, route patients and messages to you,
  deliver notifications and show your colleagues your name and availability.
* To keep it secure: detect and prevent misuse, enforce your organization's
  security settings, and investigate incidents.
* To meet legal obligations: HIPAA audit logging and record retention, and
  responses to lawful requests.
* To support and improve the Service: answer your requests and fix problems.
  Where we analyse usage to improve the product, we use aggregated or
  de-identified information.

**We never sell or rent personal information, never use it for advertising,
and never use patient information for any purpose other than providing the
Service to the Customer.** Patient information is never used to train
artificial-intelligence models.

## 5. Notifications

Push notifications and text messages sent by DocTurn are deliberately
**content-free**: they say that something needs your attention (for example
"New STAT message") and never contain patient details. You read the content
only after signing in.

## 6. When we share information

We share information only:

* **With your organization.** Your administrators can see your account, your
  activity and the audit trail of what you accessed, as HIPAA requires.
* **With service providers** who host and operate the Service on our behalf,
  under written contracts that limit their use of it and, where they could
  encounter patient information, a Business Associate Agreement. The current
  list is published at `/subprocessors`.
* **With integrations your organization turns on**, such as its scheduling
  system or electronic health record, and only as that organization directs.
* **For legal reasons**: to comply with law, a court order or a lawful request
  from a government authority; to protect the rights, safety or property of
  patients, users, Customers or DocTurn; or in connection with a merger or sale
  of DocTurn's business, subject to this Policy and to every Business Associate
  Agreement in force.

## 7. Optional artificial-intelligence features

DocTurn can extract structured fields from an admission note. By default this
runs entirely inside DocTurn. An organization may choose to use an external AI
provider instead; DocTurn sends patient information to that provider only when
the organization has turned the feature on **and** a Business Associate
Agreement with that provider is in place. That information is not used to
train the provider's models.

## 8. How long we keep information

* **Account details** are kept while your account exists, then deleted or
  de-identified as your organization directs, except where we must keep them
  longer for the records below.
* **Messages and patient information** are kept for the period your
  organization sets, and returned or destroyed when its contract ends, as its
  Business Associate Agreement requires.
* **Audit and access records** are kept for six years, the period HIPAA
  requires for documentation of compliance.

## 9. Security

We protect information with safeguards designed to meet the HIPAA Security
Rule, including encryption of data in transit, hashed passwords, optional and
organization-required two-factor authentication, automatic sign-out after
inactivity, per-organization data separation, role-based access controls and
audit logging of every access to patient information. No system is perfectly
secure. If we learn of a breach affecting your information we will notify the
affected organization and you as the law requires.

## 10. Where information is stored

DocTurn stores production information in the United States with

## 11. Your choices and rights

* **Access and correction.** Most account details are managed by your
  organization's administrator; ask them, or contact us.
* **Notifications.** You can turn notifications off in your device settings or
  in DocTurn's settings; you will then not be alerted to STAT messages.
* **Deletion.** Because your account belongs to your organization, deletion
  requests go through it. Audit records that HIPAA requires us to keep cannot
  be deleted before the end of the retention period.
* **State privacy rights.** Depending on where you live (for example
  California, Washington, Colorado, Virginia or Connecticut), you may have
  rights to know, access, correct or delete personal information, and to
  appeal a decision. Information that is PHI under HIPAA is generally exempt
  from those laws and remains governed by HIPAA. To exercise a right, contact
  us at the address below; we will verify your request and will not
  discriminate against you for making it.

## 12. Children

The Service is for licensed healthcare professionals and staff. It is not
directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information
from anyone under 18 as a user.
