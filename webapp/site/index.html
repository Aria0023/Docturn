FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can we try it before we buy?

Yes. Choose "Pilot" in the form. A pilot runs under a short evaluation agreement, typically with one service, and can start on synthetic test data before any real patient information is used.

Is DocTurn HIPAA compliant?

DocTurn is designed for HIPAA-regulated use: access control, audit logging, encryption in transit, and content-free notifications. We sign a Business Associate Agreement before any real patient information is entered. Compliance is shared — your organization keeps its own policies and training.

Does it replace pagers and phone calls?

For routine and urgent team communication, yes. DocTurn is not an emergency system: for a life-threatening emergency, use your hospital's emergency procedures. See the Clinical Safety Notice.

What does it connect to?

On-call schedules from Amion or Epic (FHIR R4), single sign-on with your identity provider, and SCIM account provisioning. The census is entered in DocTurn today; automatic EHR census sync is planned.

Which devices does it run on?

Any modern browser on a workstation, plus iPhone and Android. On a phone, add DocTurn to the Home Screen to get STAT alerts and PIN unlock.

How is billing handled?

Per clinician account per month, at the price in your signed order form, invoiced monthly. Deactivated accounts stop counting.
