Before you ask.

Can we try it first?

Yes. Choose "Pilot" in the form. A pilot runs for a fixed term with one service under a short evaluation agreement, and can start on test data before any real patient information is used.

Is DocTurn HIPAA compliant?

Yes. DocTurn is built for HIPAA-regulated messaging in acute care: access controls, a complete audit trail, encryption in transit, and lock-screen alerts with no patient details. We sign a Business Associate Agreement with every hospital before any real patient information is entered, and your hospital keeps its own policies and training.

Does it replace pagers?

For team communication, yes. DocTurn is not an emergency system: for a life-threatening emergency, follow your hospital's emergency procedures. See the Clinical Safety Notice.

What does it work with?

Your on-call schedule, your identity provider for single sign-on, and automatic account provisioning. We confirm the details for your hospital during onboarding.

Which devices?

Any modern browser on a workstation, plus iPhone and Android.
