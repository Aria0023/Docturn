Launch from your EHR

This app is opened by Epic, Oracle Health, athenahealth, MEDITECH or any SMART-enabled EHR with a patient already selected. You can also start here and pick the patient in the EHR.

How the EHR launch works

EHR → /smart/launch?iss=<fhir base>&launch=<token> → authorize at the EHR (PKCE) → /smart/callback → this page with patient and encounter context. Tokens stay in the server session and are discarded when you end the session.
